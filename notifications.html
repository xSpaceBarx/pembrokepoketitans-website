Get PokéTitans Alerts

Enable notifications once, then choose exactly which PokéTitans reminders and Pokémon GO alerts you want to receive.

🤖

Android

Enable PokéTitans push notifications on your Android device. You can also install PokéTitans to your Home Screen for quick, app-style access.

💻

Desktop

Receive PokéTitans alerts on your Mac or Windows computer.

📱

iPhone / iPad

PokéTitans must be added to your Home Screen as a web app before iPhone/iPad alerts can be enabled.

  1. Open PokéTitans in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (or More → Share), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Make sure Open as Web App is turned on, then tap Add.
  4. Open PokéTitans from the new Home Screen icon.
  5. Return to this Alerts page, choose your preferences below, tap Save My Preferences, and allow notifications when prompted.
